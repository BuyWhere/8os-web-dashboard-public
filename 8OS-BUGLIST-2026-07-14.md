### P2 [UX] Sources page contradicts Google Calendar access scope (read-only vs read+write)
- Page/URL: https://8os.ai/settings/sources and the Google OAuth URL https://accounts.google.com/... (reached from Connect Google Calendar)
- Repro: Open Settings → Sources, read the "What 8os reads" list, then click Connect Google Calendar and inspect the OAuth request URL.
- Expected: The requested Google OAuth scope and the copy on the Sources page should match. If two-way sync is claimed, the copy should say so; if read-only is claimed, the scope should be read-only.
- Actual: The Sources page explicitly states "Access is read-only. 8os never creates, edits or deletes events in your Google Calendar." but the OAuth request contains `scope=https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events` (read+write), and the card text says "Two-way sync ... events you create in 8os appear in your Google Calendar."
- Evidence: VidMee analysis of cal-sources-page screenshot (asset vidmee://asset/vidmee_img_3ce5d4654e3e55af5aa71dbf); Google OAuth URL captured from browser navigation shows scope=https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (screenshot public URL https://litter.catbox.moe/5edtlj.jpg).
- Fix: Decide the intended access model and align both the copy and the OAuth scope: either keep the page accurate by requesting a read-only scope and removing write claims, or update the privacy/copy to disclose read+write.
